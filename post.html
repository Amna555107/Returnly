📋 Posting tips

  • 📸 Add a photo. Items with photos get 5× more responses.
  • 📍 Area only. Never post your home address — a neighbourhood is enough.
  • 🔍 Be specific. Colour, brand, model number — unique details matter.
  • 🤝 Stay safe. Meet in a public place when arranging returns.
  • ✅ Mark returned. Once reunited, update your post from your dashboard.
