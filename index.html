Our Story

Built after losing a cat 🐱

Returnly was born from a very personal moment of panic. Our founder, Amna, woke up one morning and found that her cat, Berbal, was missing from home. She searched the whole neighbourhood, but she couldn't find him, and she felt sad and helpless.

"What I needed was a simple place where the whole community could help, and where anyone who found something could let the owner know."

Berbal was returned by someone who had mistakenly taken him, thinking he was their own cat. Amna was lucky, and she wants everyone to have that chance. Returnly is that notice board, just online and open to everyone. Because no one should have to search alone.

Your safety matters

Returnly never asks for or displays full home addresses. We only show the area or neighbourhood where an item was lost or found. Contact details are only visible to signed-in users, keeping spammers out and your privacy intact.
